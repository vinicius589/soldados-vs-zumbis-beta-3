# Prompts finais — reconstrução retrô da Beta 4

As artes desta rodada foram criadas com o gerador de imagens integrado e
ligadas ao motor por folhas de oito poses. Todas usam fundo transparente,
caixa constante, contato fixo com o terreno e direção coerente do combate.

## Defensores (uma folha 8x8 por região)

> Authentic crisp 16-bit arcade pixel art, transparent background, exact 8x8
> grid, no labels or borders, generous padding, all defenders facing right,
> fixed feet or keel baseline and constant scale. Columns: idle inhale, idle
> exhale, grounded movement contact, movement passing pose, action
> anticipation, action with believable recoil and no projectile, realistic
> reload or service, concise damage reaction. No jumping, clipping, bullets,
> shell casings, gradients or antialiasing. Human motion is physically
> grounded; regional uniforms and equipment remain distinct.

Elencos usados: polícia/SWAT de Nova York; forças egípcias de clima desértico;
Marinha do Brasil, estação móvel, barco e submarino em Minas Gerais.

## Infectados (uma folha 8x12 por região)

> Authentic crisp 16-bit arcade pixel art, transparent background, exact 8x12
> grid, no labels or borders, wide padding, every zombie facing left, constant
> scale and fixed ground or water contact. Columns: idle A, idle B, locomotion
> contact, passing pose, attack wind-up, attack contact, damage reaction,
> special ability or recovery. Grotesque fantasy is welcome, but weight,
> limping, dragging, swimming and lunging must remain biomechanically readable.
> No floating, missing limbs, cross-cell fragments, gradients or antialiasing.

Os doze tipos de cada região preservam a lore Hélix, Khepra e Cachoeira.

## Mulher de alerta da Cachoeira (1x8)

> Brazilian rescue coordinator in navy-blue and orange, transparent authentic
> 16-bit pixel art, fixed feet baseline and constant scale. Worried idle,
> notices danger, opens both arms, begins shouting, loud warning, second loud
> warning, lowers arms, returns to idle. Complete hands and mouth, no salute,
> no speech bubble or text inside the sheet.

## Correção do cenário da Cachoeira

> Preserve the approved 1280x720 composition and change only the first/top
> reddish land lane: keep it continuous, level and full-width to the
> waterfall-side entrance, matching the usable width of the last/bottom land
> lane. Remove taper or rocky obstruction. Do not add characters, bombs, UI,
> text, flags, doors or scenery; keep both central water lanes unchanged.
